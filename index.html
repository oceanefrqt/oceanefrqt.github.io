About Me

I'm a PhD candidate with a passion for computer science, data analysis, and biomedical research. Currently, I'm working on developing classification models to predict treatment response in cancer patients, using transcriptomic data as my foundation. This research has not only deepened my understanding of the complex relationships between genes, diseases, and treatments, but also sparked an interest with the potential of data-driven approaches to improve human health. My long-term goal is to apply my skills to neuro-oncology, where I hope to contribute to the development of more effective and personalized therapies for brain cancer patients. Outside of my academic pursuits, I'm a creative at heart and enjoy expressing myself through various art forms, including reading, painting, and drawing.
